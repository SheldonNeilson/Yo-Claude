The six characters are short clips made with AI image-to-video tools (ComfyUI, Qwen-Image-Edit) from free-licence stock photos on
[Pexels](https://www.pexels.com/license/), tidied (plain backdrop, even light) and then animated. The people in the photos have no
connection with this project, did not take part in it and do not endorse it.

that the people shown said, did or approve anything. Use the character settings to pick one, or replace them with your own.
If you are the person in one of these photos and want its character removed, open an issue and it will be taken out.
